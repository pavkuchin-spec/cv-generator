# Corporate data warehouse & BI integration (DECO Systems)

## Problem
The client needed a corporate data warehouse integrated with BI reporting, built to a consistent architectural standard, delivered by a cross-functional team under a fixed budget and timeline.

## What I did
- Led a cross-functional team of 6 analysts and engineers as analytics team lead on the project, establishing development standards and best practices for the team.
- Implemented the Data Vault 2.0 methodology across 100+ tables spanning 6 source systems as the warehouse's core modeling architecture.
- Owned delivery of the corporate data warehouse end to end.

## Scale & outcome
Delivered the corporate data warehouse on time and within budget, covering 100+ tables across 6 source systems, with BI reporting integrated on top.

## Result lenses
- **Scale** — 100+ tables across 6 source systems; a cross-functional team of 6 analysts and engineers.
- **Help** — the team of 6, who got development standards and best practices established to work to; BI reporting consumers, who got reporting integrated on top of the delivered warehouse.
- **Problems** — the absence of a consistent architectural standard, addressed by adopting Data Vault 2.0 as the warehouse's core modeling architecture across all 6 source systems; and delivery risk under a fixed budget and timeline, which the project met.
- **Leadership** — led the cross-functional team of 6 as analytics team lead and owned delivery of the corporate data warehouse end to end.

## Technologies
Data Vault 2.0, data warehouse architecture, BI reporting integration, team leadership.
